Why You Should Choose Stack’s Bowers Galleries

Founded in 1933, Stack’s Bowers Galleries is America’s oldest rare coin auctioneer and dealership, renowned for excellence, expertise, and record-breaking auction results. We specialize in U.S., world, and ancient coins, tokens, medals, and currency, offering tailored services for seasoned collectors, novice enthusiasts, and astute investors. Whether you are looking to sell or buy coins, our team of coin experts provides personalized attention to every client, ensuring accessibility and dedication regardless of collection size. In addition to auctions, we offer direct sales, direct purchase offers and appraisals complemented by online platforms and educational resources. Unlike other coin auction sites, we blend tradition with innovation, which is why Stack’s Bowers Galleries continues to lead the numismatic industry.

About Stack's Bowers Galleries

As America’s oldest rare coin auction house, Stack’s Bowers Galleries has auctioned many of the rarest coins and most valuable collections ever assembled. Now, more than ninety years after we held our first coin auction, our tradition of success has carried forward into the 21st century as we continue to realize top market prices for rare coins and currency for collectors around the world. Our knowledgeable staff offers unsurpassed expertise in all areas of numismatics, as well as a commitment to excellent personal service.

If this is your first time visiting our website and you are considering selling a single coin or an entire collection, please call or email us with any questions. If you are researching the value of your coins, we recommend checking out our coin auction archives. You can schedule a consignment, purchase, or appraisal consultation through our store appraisal form. As reputable coin dealers, we have more than a dozen locations worldwide. If you are nearby, please feel free to schedule an appointment with our expert staff.

By leveraging our global network of dealers, our auctions deliver endless possibilities for buyers. Be sure to view our upcoming coin auctions to participate. Don’t forget that we also buy and sell coins, currency and bullion privately daily!

Introduction to Our Auctions

How do coin and currency auctions work?

At Stack’s Bowers Galleries, coin and currency auctions happen both in person and online and follow a simple process. Register as a bidder, explore the auction catalog to see what is available. Participate in the weeks before by submitting bids online or via bid sheet or on the day of the event by placing bids either in the live auction room or via livestream

How do I sell my coins in an auction?

Selling coins at Stack’s Bowers Galleries begins with a consultation with a consignment specialist to determine your collection’s value, the best venue to offer the property and the economic terms of consignment. After receiving your consignment, your items are expertly cataloged, marketed, and featured in an upcoming auction or you can sell coins directly to us. Payment terms are transparent, ensuring a smooth and rewarding selling process.

Can I find out how much my coins are worth before listing them in an auction?

Yes, Stack’s Bowers Galleries offers professional coin appraisals to evaluate your coins before auction. Our experts provide accurate valuations based on market trends, helping you decide the best approach for selling your collection.

Is it worth buying or selling coins in an auction?

Yes. Auctions provide competitive opportunities for both buyers and sellers, often achieving top market prices. With Stack’s Bowers Galleries’ expertise, global reach, and unmatched resources, our coin auctions are known for delivering industry leading prizes realized. Visit our upcoming auction schedule to see what we have coming up.

When is Stack’s Bowers Galleries’ next coin auction?

We run auctions frequently featuring U.S., world, and ancient coins, rare paper money and specialty collectibles. We also run weekly Precious Metals Auctions on Thursdays. You can view our upcoming auctions calendar to see the next coin auction. Join our online coin auctions for an easy and convenient bidding experience.

Registering is quick and easy through our registration page. We recommend you sign up early to browse lots, set your bid limit, and ensure a hassle-free auction experience.
